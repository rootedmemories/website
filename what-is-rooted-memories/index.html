What is Rooted Memories?

Rooted Memories is a private family archive built around people. It gives your family one place to keep photos and stories connected to the people they belong to, so memories stay clearer, easier to return to, and more meaningful over time.

What it is

It is not a social platform, a group chat, or a folder system. It is a family archive designed to hold memories in a structure your family can understand.

What it is built around

The archive is organised around your family tree. Each person has their own place, and memories are kept with the people they relate to.

What that changes

When photos and stories stay connected to people, the archive becomes easier to follow. Memories keep more of their context, and your family spends less time guessing where things belong or what they mean.

Who it is for

It is for families who want a clearer and more lasting way to keep photos, stories, and family history together.

What it becomes

At first, it gives your family a clearer place to keep memories. Over time, it becomes a private record of family life built gradually in one place.

Your family has the photos.

Here is a place to collect them, and put them on display safely.

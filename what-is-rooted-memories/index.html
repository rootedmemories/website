What is Rooted Memories?

Rooted Memories is a private family archive built around people. It gives your family one place to keep photographs and stories connected to the people they belong to, so memories remain clearer, easier to return to and more meaningful over time.

It is not another social platform, group chat or folder system. It is a place designed to keep people, memories and family history connected.

01 What it is

A private archive for your family.

Rooted Memories is designed to hold family photographs, captions and stories in a structure your family can understand.

Instead of adding another stream of posts or another collection of folders, it gives those memories a permanent place within your family itself.

02 What it is built around

Your family tree gives the archive its structure.

Every person has their own place. Photographs, captions and stories are kept with the family members they relate to, rather than disappearing into a camera roll containing thousands of unrelated images.

That changes the basic question from "Where should this file go?" to "Who does this memory belong to?"

03 What that changes

Memories keep more of their meaning.

A photograph can survive long after the context around it has disappeared. Names are forgotten. Dates become uncertain. Stories that were once obvious to one generation mean very little to the next.

By keeping memories connected to people, Rooted Memories makes the archive easier to understand and gives future generations a better chance of knowing what they are looking at and why it mattered.

Family history makes more sense when people remain at the centre of it.

Rooted Memories does not treat your family as a collection of files. The people come first. The photographs, stories and memories build around them.

What it becomes

More than somewhere to put photographs.

At first, Rooted Memories gives your family a clearer place to keep memories.

Over time, as photographs, captions and stories accumulate, each person's profile begins to form a lasting record of their life.

Together, those individual records become a private history of your family built gradually across generations.

Who it is for

For families who want their memories to remain understandable.

01

Families with photographs spread across multiple phones

Bring separate collections together without turning them into another unstructured pile of files.

02

Families living apart

Give relatives one private place where everyone can contribute to the same family history.

03

Parents preserving childhood

Keep photographs and stories somewhere children can return to when they are older.

04

Families protecting older memories

Preserve photographs, names and stories before the people who remember their context are no longer here to explain them.

Built to grow without becoming complicated.

A family archive should become richer with time, not harder to use. Rooted Memories keeps the experience straightforward while the history underneath it becomes deeper.

What Rooted Memories actually is

Rooted Memories is a private family archive where your photos and stories are organised by the people they belong to — not lost across apps, devices, and group chats.

At its core

Most families don’t lose their memories in one moment.

They lose them slowly — across camera rolls, WhatsApp groups, old phones, cloud folders, and forgotten accounts.

Rooted Memories brings everything into one place, structured properly around your family.

What makes it different

This isn’t another photo storage app.

Every memory is attached to a person in your family tree. That means everything stays organised naturally over time.

Instead of scrolling endlessly, you can go straight to a person and see their life build out properly.

The bigger idea

This starts as a way to organise your family’s photos today.

But over time, it becomes something much more valuable — a full family history, built in real time, that can be passed down.

Not just photos. Context. Stories. People. Meaning.

Why it matters

Having access to thousands of photos isn’t the same as understanding your family’s story.

Rooted Memories turns scattered moments into something clear, structured, and actually meaningful to look back on.

Your family already has the memories.

This is where they finally come together.

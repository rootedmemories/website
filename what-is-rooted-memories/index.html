What is Rooted Memories?

Rooted Memories is a private place for your family's memories, built around the people who make them. Families can share photographs and stories privately, keep them organised around each person, and gradually build something their children and future generations can return to.

Your family's memories deserve somewhere of their own. Not a public feed. Not a forgotten camera roll. A place built around your family.

01 What it is

A private place for family life.

Rooted Memories gives your family somewhere to keep the photographs, captions and stories that matter to you.

Instead of becoming another stream of posts or another folder full of unnamed files, those memories become part of a living family archive that everyone you invite can contribute to.

02 Built around people

Your family tree gives the memories their structure.

Every person has their own place. Photographs, captions and stories remain connected to the family members they relate to instead of disappearing among thousands of unrelated images.

It means a photograph is not simply another file. It becomes part of someone's story.

03 Keep the meaning

A photograph means more when its story survives with it.

Camera rolls grow quickly. Years later, the photograph may still exist while the names, places, dates and stories surrounding it have already begun to disappear.

Rooted Memories keeps photographs connected to the people and context that give them meaning, so the next generation has a better chance of understanding what they are looking at.

Private by design

Not every family photograph needs an audience.

Families should be able to share the everyday parts of life without needing to make them public.

Rooted Memories gives parents, grandparents and relatives a private place to share photographs and memories with the people they personally choose to invite into their family tree.

A photograph shared with family does not simply disappear into a message thread afterwards. It can remain part of the person's archive for years to come.

Imagine giving your child the story of their own life.

Childhood produces thousands of photographs, but very few children eventually receive a clear record of it. Rooted Memories gives families somewhere to build that record gradually, so one day a child can look back through their own life, the people around them and the family they grew up within.

Built to be handed down

An archive that becomes more valuable as your family adds to it.

At first, Rooted Memories gives your family somewhere clearer to keep photographs and stories.

As the years pass, each person's collection grows into a richer record of their life.

Parents add childhood. Grandparents add older memories. Relatives contribute stories and photographs held on their own phones and in their own albums.

Together, those individual lives begin to form something much larger: a private family history that can continue beyond the people who first created it.

Made for families

Different generations can use it for different reasons.

01

Parents sharing childhood privately

Keep grandparents and relatives involved as children grow without needing to make every family moment public.

02

Grandparents holding older memories

Add photographs and stories from earlier generations while the people who remember their meaning can still explain them.

03

Families whose photographs are everywhere

Bring together memories spread across different phones, camera rolls, albums and relatives without creating another unstructured pile of files.

04

Children who will one day inherit it

Give them somewhere to rediscover their childhood, see the people who surrounded them and understand how their family fits together.

05

Families serious about preserving their history

Build a deeper archive of people, photographs, relationships and stories that remains useful long after today's camera rolls and devices have changed.

Built to grow without becoming complicated.

Rooted Memories can begin with today's photographs and a few people in your family. The structure underneath it allows the archive to become deeper naturally, without turning the experience into specialist genealogy software.

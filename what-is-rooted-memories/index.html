What is Rooted Memories

What is Rooted Memories?

Rooted Memories is a private family archive that organises photos and stories around the people they belong to. Instead of keeping memories scattered across phones, chats, albums, and folders, it gives your family one clear place to keep them together.

How it works

Each person in your family has their own place in the tree. Photos and stories are added to the people they belong to, so the structure of your family becomes the structure of the archive.

That makes memories easier to understand, easier to return to, and less likely to lose their context over time.

What problem it solves

Family memories often end up spread across phones, chats, albums, old boxes, and forgotten folders. Even when the photos still exist, the story around them is often unclear or gradually lost.

Rooted Memories brings old and new memories together in one private place, so they stay organised around the people who matter most.

Why it matters

When memories are kept properly, they remain easier for your family to understand and return to over time. Instead of a collection of disconnected images, they become part of a clearer family story.

Your family already has the memories

This is where they can be kept properly.

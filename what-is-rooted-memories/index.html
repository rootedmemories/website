What is Rooted Memories?

What is
Rooted Memories?

Rooted Memories is a private place for families to keep their photographs together. You create a family tree, tag the people in your photos and add captions when there's something worth remembering. Relatives you invite can add their pictures too.

How it works

Start with the people
in your family.

01 / The family tree

Each person has their own gallery.

Add people to your family tree and tag them in photographs. A picture with several relatives in it can appear in each of their galleries. You can still see everything together in the main gallery.

02 / Photos and captions

Keep the details with the photo.

You can write a caption with a name, a place or what was happening that day. Things that seem obvious now aren't always easy to work out twenty years later.

03 / Inviting family

Your relatives can add their photos too.

Invite family members to your private tree. They might have pictures you've never seen, or remember names and dates you've forgotten. Everyone can add what they have.

Old photos and new ones

We're making family history
as we go.

The photos you're taking of your children, parents and everyday family life will be the old photographs someone looks through one day. You can add them now, while you still remember the details.

And if you find an old album or a relative sends you photographs from years ago, those can go in the same place. You don't have to sort through everything before you start.

How can we help?

Whether you need help with your account, your family tree, joining Rooted Memories or simply have a question about the app, you can contact us directly.

We will do our best to get you the help you need.

General & support

Get in touch.

Email the Rooted Memories support team and include as much information as you can about your question or issue.

support@rootedmemories.co.uk

We aim to respond within 72 hours.

Support

What we can help with.

Account help

Help accessing your Rooted Memories account, signing in or resolving problems with your profile.

Family trees

Questions about your family tree, invitations, family members or managing your archive.

Technical support

If something in Rooted Memories is not working as expected, let us know what happened and which device you are using.

General enquiries

Questions about Rooted Memories, how the private family archive works or anything else you would like to ask us.

Having a technical problem? When contacting us, including the email address associated with your Rooted Memories account, your device type and a short description of what happened can help us investigate the issue more quickly.

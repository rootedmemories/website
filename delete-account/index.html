Account deletion

Delete your Rooted Memories account.

You can request permanent deletion of your Rooted Memories account at any time. Send your request to our support address and we will process the deletion within 72 hours.

Request deletion

Email our support team.

Send the request from, or identify, the account you want deleted so we can process the correct account.

support@rootedmemories.co.uk
What happens

What happens when your account is deleted.

Your account

Your Rooted Memories account and associated profile data will be permanently deleted.

Your uploads

Photos and other media uploaded by your account will be permanently deleted.

Family trees

Deleting your account does not delete photographs or other content contributed by other members of a family tree.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are processed within 72 hours.

Account deletion is permanent. Once the deletion has been completed, your deleted account data and uploaded media cannot be restored through Rooted Memories.

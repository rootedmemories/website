Start by creating your tree

When you open Rooted Memories, tap Get Started. This will allow you to create your account and begin setting up your family tree as the admin.

As the admin, you are responsible for creating the first tree, adding family members, uploading memories, and inviting relatives to join.

iPhone steps

  1. Tap Install on iPhone above.
  2. Install TestFlight if prompted.
  3. Open the Rooted Memories TestFlight page.
  4. Tap Install.
  5. Open Rooted Memories.
  6. Tap Get Started.
  7. Create your account using the email address you signed up with.
  8. Create your family tree.

Android steps

  1. Tap Download Android APK above.
  2. Download the APK file.
  3. Open the downloaded file.
  4. If Android blocks the install, tap Settings.
  5. Allow installation from this source.
  6. Return to the APK and tap Install.
  7. Open Rooted Memories.
  8. Tap Get Started.
  9. Create your account using the email address you signed up with.
  10. Create your family tree.

Invite your family from inside the app

After creating your tree, invite relatives directly from Rooted Memories. This helps connect each person to the correct family tree and keeps the invitation process simple.

Do not send family members your own login details. Instead, add them to the tree and use the in-app invite flow so they can join with their own account.

Step 1 Add or select the family member you would like to invite.
Step 2 Send the invitation directly from inside Rooted Memories.

Having trouble?

  • Make sure you tapped Get Started, not Log In.
  • Use the email address you signed up with.
  • If you are on Android, make sure the APK has fully installed before opening the app.
  • Invite family members from inside the app rather than sharing your own account details.

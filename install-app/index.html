What is Rooted Memories?

Rooted Memories helps families preserve photos, stories and memories in one private place.

Instead of losing important moments across phones, group chats, social media and old devices, everything stays connected to the people they belong to.

Build your family tree

  • Create a private family tree.
  • Add parents, grandparents and relatives.
  • Give every person their own place in the archive.
  • Grow your family history over time.

Preserve memories forever

  • Store photos and family stories.
  • Keep memories organised around real people.
  • Invite relatives to contribute.
  • Build something future generations can explore.

Getting started

  1. Download Rooted Memories.
  2. Create your free account.
  3. Start your family tree.
  4. Add your first family members.
  5. Upload photos and memories.
  6. Invite relatives to join and contribute.

Private by design

Rooted Memories is not public social media. Your family archive is private, family-focused and built around the people in your tree.

Early founding families receive free lifetime access while the app is in early access.

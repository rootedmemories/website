ROOTED MEMORIES – PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 27 October 2026

Company: ROOTED MEMORIES LTD

Contact: support@rootedmemories.com

1. Introduction and Who We Are

ROOTED MEMORIES LTD ("Rooted Memories", "we", "us" or "our") operates the Rooted Memories mobile application and associated website and services.

Rooted Memories is a private family archive platform that enables families to organise, preserve and share memories through family Trees, individual person records known as Leaves, photographs and associated information.

ROOTED MEMORIES LTD is the data controller responsible for personal information processed for the operation, administration and security of the platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, who may access it, how long we retain it and the rights available to individuals under applicable data protection law.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

For privacy enquiries, requests or complaints, contact: support@rootedmemories.com .

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Profile Information

When you register, access or manage an account, we may process:

  • Your name or display name.
  • Your email address and/or telephone number.
  • Verification information and authentication records.
  • Your profile photograph and date of birth, where provided.
  • Account identifiers, registration dates, status and account settings.
  • Information about your Tree memberships, permissions and administrative roles.

Users may register using an email address, telephone number or supported combination of these methods.

2.2 Family Trees and Leaves

The platform stores information created by users within private family Trees, including:

  • Tree names and structures.
  • Leaves representing individual people.
  • Names, photographs, dates of birth and family relationships, where provided.
  • Connections between Leaves and registered accounts.
  • Tree membership, administrative permissions and relevant activity timestamps.

A Leaf may represent a person who has not registered for a Rooted Memories account. Information about that person may have been supplied by another family member.

2.3 Photographs and Other Memories

When users upload content, we process the information necessary to store, organise and display that content, including:

  • Uploaded photographs and supported media.
  • Captions and descriptions.
  • Dates and other metadata associated with uploaded content.
  • Upload timestamps and identifiers of the uploading account.
  • Associations between media and one or more Leaves within a Tree.

Where supplied and retained, image metadata may include details such as capture dates or other information embedded in a file.

Media is stored within the relevant Tree and may be associated with multiple Leaves. A photograph attached to several Leaves within the same Tree is not necessarily stored as a separate photograph for each association.

2.4 Subscription Information

To administer free trials, subscriptions and access permissions, we process:

  • Trial start dates and expiry dates.
  • Subscription status, product and billing period.
  • Purchase and transaction identifiers.
  • Subscription activation, renewal, cancellation and expiry information.
  • Whether a Tree has previously had a successful paid subscription.
  • Whether a Tree has grandfathered status.
  • The account responsible for administering a Tree's subscription.

Subscriptions are processed through the applicable Apple App Store or Google Play billing system.

We receive the transaction and entitlement information needed to provide the service. We do not receive your complete payment card number from Apple or Google.

2.5 Technical and Usage Information

When you use the platform, technical information may be processed, including:

  • IP addresses.
  • Device, operating system and app version information.
  • Technical identifiers required for authentication and app operation.
  • Error reports, diagnostic information and security logs.
  • Relevant app activity and event timestamps.
  • Push notification tokens and notification interaction events.

2.6 Invitations and Communications

We may process contact information supplied when users invite family members, together with the information necessary to send invitations and account verification messages.

If you contact our support team, we also process your correspondence and any information reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry.

3. Where Information Comes From

We receive personal information from:

  • You, when you register or use the service.
  • Other members who add you to a Tree, create a Leaf representing you, upload memories or record family relationships.
  • App stores and subscription management services, when relevant to purchases and subscription status.
  • Technical service providers supporting authentication, delivery, security and platform operation.

Because Rooted Memories is a collaborative family archive, some information about you may be provided by other members rather than collected directly from you.

Where applicable law requires additional notification to an individual, we will take reasonable steps to provide it, subject to lawful exceptions.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We process personal information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Verifying identity and managing secure account access.
  • Creating, displaying and maintaining private family Trees and Leaves.
  • Storing, organising and displaying photographs and associated memories.
  • Enabling permitted family collaboration, invitations and relationships.
  • Managing trials, subscriptions and read-only access.
  • Applying Tree retention and deletion rules.
  • Sending operational notifications, account messages and relevant warnings.
  • Providing customer support and responding to privacy or deletion requests.
  • Detecting misuse, investigating technical problems and maintaining security.
  • Understanding service performance and, where legally permitted, measuring registration and purchase conversions.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, photographs or family memories.

We do not use the contents of your private family archive to build advertising profiles or target advertisements based on your family relationships or memories.

6. Private Trees and Access to Content

Rooted Memories is designed around private family Trees.

Information and memories within a Tree are available to users who have been granted access to that Tree, subject to the permissions available in the application.

Tree Administrators can manage Tree structures, membership and certain content in accordance with the application's permissions.

Other authorised members may add or manage memories and information where their permissions allow.

Content is not made publicly discoverable merely because it has been uploaded to a private Tree.

Users should only upload or share information about other people where they have an appropriate basis to do so, and should respect the privacy and rights of other individuals.

If you believe information about you has been uploaded inappropriately, contact us so that we can review the matter.

7. Children and Sensitive Information

Rooted Memories is intended to help families preserve memories across generations. Trees and Leaves may therefore contain information about children, including names, photographs, dates of birth and relationships.

Such information may be added by a parent, guardian or family member with appropriate authority or another lawful basis.

Children's information is subject to appropriate privacy safeguards. We take particular care when assessing requests or concerns involving children.

Family memories may also contain sensitive personal information, including information about health or other private circumstances.

Users should exercise particular care before uploading sensitive information about another person and must not upload content that unlawfully infringes their rights.

We do not use uploaded photographs to identify individuals through facial recognition or to infer sensitive characteristics for advertising purposes.

Parents, guardians and individuals with concerns about a child's information may contact support@rootedmemories.com.

8. Free Trials, Subscriptions and Tree Retention

Rooted Memories operates a Tree-based subscription system. Access and retention rules apply to the relevant Tree rather than automatically to every Tree associated with a user.

8.1 Fourteen-Day Free Trial

Eligible new Trees receive a 14-day free trial with access to the available editing and upload features.

If the trial expires without an active subscription, the Tree becomes read-only.

Members may continue viewing the Tree during the applicable retention period, but uploading and editing are restricted.

8.2 Never-Paid Trees

Important: A non-grandfathered Tree that has never had a successful paid subscription is scheduled for permanent deletion 30 days after its 14-day free trial ends, unless a qualifying subscription is activated before deletion.

When this deletion occurs, the Tree and its associated media are removed from the active service.

This includes content contributed by other users to that Tree.

We may send trial-expiry notifications and a final deletion warning before the scheduled removal.

Notification delivery is not guaranteed. Users remain responsible for monitoring the trial and subscription status shown in the app.

8.3 Trees Previously Paid For

Once a Tree has had a successful paid subscription, that Tree is permanently exempt from automatic deletion under the never-paid trial rule.

If its subscription later expires, the Tree becomes read-only, but its memories remain preserved within the service.

Cancellation or expiry of a previously paid subscription does not, by itself, cause the Tree to be automatically deleted.

This protection does not prevent deletion requested by an authorised person, removal of individual content under applicable Tree permissions, or processing required by law.

8.4 Grandfathered Trees

Certain Trees created during the pre-release testing period have grandfathered status.

These Trees are not treated as ordinary never-paid trial Trees and are not subject to automatic deletion under the unpaid-trial rule.

Grandfathered status and successful payment history are recorded separately within the service.

8.5 Read-Only Does Not Mean Deleted

Read-only access means a Tree may remain visible while editing and uploading are restricted.

For never-paid Trees, read-only status is temporary and may end in permanent deletion under section 8.2.

For previously paid or grandfathered Trees, read-only status does not by itself trigger automatic deletion.

9. Media Ownership, Removal and Deletion

The application records which account uploaded each media item.

A media item may be attached to multiple Leaves within the same Tree.

9.1 Removing Media from a Leaf

Removing a media item from one Leaf removes that particular association.

The media remains available where it is still associated with another Leaf within the same Tree.

Where a media item has no remaining Leaf associations, it is permanently removed from that Tree.

9.2 Leaving or Being Removed from a Tree

If a user leaves a Tree, is unlinked from a Tree, or is removed from it, all media uploaded by that user within that Tree is permanently removed.

This applies even where those uploads were also attached to other Leaves within the same Tree.

Media uploaded by other users is not automatically deleted solely because one member leaves.

9.3 Deleting a Leaf

Tree Administrators can delete Leaves in accordance with the application's permissions.

Deleting an unassigned Leaf removes its associations with media. Media that remains associated with another Leaf is retained.

Deleting a Leaf assigned to a registered user may also remove that user from the Tree, resulting in deletion of that user's uploads within the Tree.

9.4 No Recovery Facility

Important: The current version does not provide a Recently Deleted folder, user-facing recovery area or restoration feature for content removed under these rules.

Users should carefully review deletion warnings before confirming an irreversible action.

10. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Rooted Memories account by contacting:

support@rootedmemories.com

We may take reasonable steps to verify that a deletion request is genuine before processing it.

Account deletion is administered through our account management systems.

When an account is deleted:

  • Its authentication credentials, tokens and account profile are removed.
  • The account loses access to all Trees.
  • Media uploaded by that account is permanently removed across all Trees in which it appears.
  • Associated media captions and metadata are removed with those uploads.
  • Tree memberships and account links are removed.

Deleting an account does not automatically delete entire Trees, other members' uploaded content, or every Leaf that may represent the deleted individual.

Where the deleted account was the Tree Administrator, the affected Tree may become read-only. Applicable Tree retention rules continue to apply.

We process verified deletion requests without undue delay and within applicable legal requirements.

Limited records may be retained where required by law, or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Deletion from the active service is irreversible. Any residual copies contained in restricted technical backups, where maintained, are subject to the normal backup retention and removal process.

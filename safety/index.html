ROOTED MEMORIES - CHILD SAFETY STANDARDS

Effective Date: 27 October 2026

Company: ROOTED MEMORIES LTD

Child Safety Contact: support@rootedmemories.com

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

ROOTED MEMORIES LTD ("Rooted Memories", "we", "us" or "our") is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse, exploitation and other unlawful harm.

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Rooted Memories is designed to help families preserve photographs, relationships and memories in private family archives.

The safety, privacy and dignity of children are important considerations in how the platform is operated and how safety concerns are addressed.

These standards apply to all Rooted Memories users and to content shared or stored through the service.

2. Family Archives and Children's Information

Rooted Memories allows authorised family members to create private family Trees.

Trees may contain Leaves representing children, together with names, photographs, dates of birth, family relationships and associated memories.

A child may be represented within a Tree without having a registered Rooted Memories account.

Tree content is intended to be accessible only to members authorised to access that Tree, subject to the permissions available in the application.

Rooted Memories does not make private family Trees publicly discoverable through the platform.

Users must respect the privacy and rights of children when creating Leaves or uploading content.

Where applicable, users must have appropriate authority or another lawful basis to share information concerning a child.

3. Strictly Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following activities and content are prohibited on Rooted Memories:

  • Creating, uploading, possessing, sharing or distributing child sexual abuse material.
  • Any content that sexually exploits or abuses a child.
  • Grooming, soliciting or attempting to establish contact with a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual coercion, blackmail, extortion or threats involving a child.
  • Sharing or threatening to share sexual images of minors.
  • Creating or distributing unlawful digitally altered or AI-generated child sexual abuse imagery.
  • Facilitating, encouraging or arranging the sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Using the service to harass, threaten, exploit or unlawfully endanger a child.
  • Using false identities, unauthorised access or other deceptive methods to facilitate harm to children.

This list is not exhaustive. Other unlawful or seriously harmful conduct may also violate our Terms of Service and applicable law.

These restrictions apply regardless of whether content is publicly accessible or shared only within a private family Tree.

4. Responsibilities of Users and Administrators

All users are expected to contribute responsibly and respect the safety, privacy and dignity of other individuals.

Users must not upload content that unlawfully infringes the rights of a child or any other person.

Tree Administrators should exercise particular care when managing membership, Leaves and access to family photographs.

Where a user becomes aware of suspected child exploitation, unlawful imagery or other serious safeguarding concerns, they should report the matter promptly.

Users must not independently investigate suspected child abuse by searching for, downloading or redistributing potentially illegal material.

5. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users, parents, guardians and other concerned individuals may report suspected child safety violations to Rooted Memories.

Reports may concern unlawful content, inappropriate behaviour, exploitation, threats or information about a child being used in a harmful or unlawful manner.

Our designated child safety contact is:

support@rootedmemories.com

5.1 Information to Include in a Report

Where available and safe to provide, a report should include:

  • A brief description of the concern.
  • The relevant Tree, account or person involved, if known.
  • The approximate date or time of the incident.
  • Any relevant non-sensitive details that may help us identify and assess the issue.

Important: Do not download, forward or attach suspected child sexual abuse material to a report or email. Describe the concern without reproducing potentially illegal content.

Individuals who do not have a Rooted Memories account may also contact us using the published email address.

6. How We Handle Safety Reports

We take reports of suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation and serious safeguarding concerns seriously.

When a concern is received, we assess the information available and determine an appropriate response based on the reported risk, available evidence and applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, our response may include:

  • Reviewing the reported concern.
  • Requesting further non-sensitive information where appropriate.
  • Restricting access to relevant content or accounts.
  • Removing prohibited or unlawful content from the service.
  • Suspending or terminating accounts involved in serious violations.
  • Preserving relevant information where necessary and lawful for safeguarding, investigation or reporting purposes.
  • Referring relevant matters to competent authorities where required or appropriate under applicable law.

Where we become aware of illegal content, we will take action in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

We may limit disclosure of investigation details where necessary to protect children, personal information, evidence or ongoing investigations.

Response times may vary according to the urgency, complexity and nature of the report.

If you wish to raise a concern about a safety-related decision, you may contact our support address and request that the matter be reviewed.

7. Cooperation with Authorities

Rooted Memories cooperates with competent law enforcement agencies and relevant regulatory authorities in accordance with applicable law.

Where legally required, we report detected and previously unreported child sexual abuse and exploitation content to the National Crime Agency (NCA) or another legally recognised reporting body.

We may preserve and disclose relevant information where required or otherwise permitted by law for safeguarding, investigation or legal proceedings.

Any handling of personal information in connection with safety concerns is subject to applicable data protection requirements.

8. Immediate Danger and External Reporting

Emergency: If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police or emergency services immediately. In the United Kingdom, call 999. Do not wait for a response from Rooted Memories.

Individuals concerned about online child sexual abuse, grooming or exploitation in the United Kingdom may also contact the National Crime Agency's Child Exploitation and Online Protection (CEOP) service.

CEOP: www.ceop.police.uk

Suspected online child sexual abuse imagery may also be reported through the Internet Watch Foundation's official reporting service.

Internet Watch Foundation: www.iwf.org.uk/report

These organisations have different reporting responsibilities. Concerns about immediate danger should be referred directly to emergency services.

9. Privacy and Protection of Information

We recognise that information concerning children may be particularly sensitive.

Safety reports and associated information will be handled with appropriate care, taking account of confidentiality, safeguarding needs and applicable law.

We may need to access or process relevant information where necessary and lawful to investigate a reported concern or comply with legal obligations.

Our Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, retain and disclose personal information, including information relating to children.

Privacy Policy: rootedmemories.com/privacy/

These Child Safety Standards supplement our Terms of Service.

Our Terms explain the general rules governing accounts, family Trees, uploaded content, acceptable use and enforcement.

These Child Safety Standards provide additional information about our approach to child sexual abuse, exploitation and related safeguarding concerns.

Nothing in these standards excludes or restricts any obligation imposed by applicable law.

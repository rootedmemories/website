Installation Guide

You've Been Invited To A Family Tree

A family member has invited you to join their Rooted Memories archive. Follow the steps below to install the app and access your invitation.

Important: Tap "Log In"

If you have been invited to an existing family tree, do not tap Get Started.

Instead, tap Log In and use the account connected to your invitation.

Download Rooted Memories

Choose your device below and install the latest version of the app.

Install on iPhone / iPad (TestFlight) Install on Android (APK)

How To Join Your Family Tree

  1. Download and install Rooted Memories using the links above.
  2. Open the app once installation is complete.
  3. Tap Log In.
  4. Enter the email address and password associated with your invitation.
  5. Your family tree should automatically appear.
  6. You can now explore family members, photos and stories.

Need Help?

If you have received an invitation but cannot access your family tree, please contact the family member who invited you.

As Rooted Memories is currently in testing, occasional updates and improvements may be released during the early access period.

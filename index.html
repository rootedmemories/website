Our purpose

A photo can last forever. Its story doesn't always.

You might have a photo of your grandparents and no idea where it was taken or who else was there, and the person who could tell you may no longer be around to ask. The same thing will happen to the photos we're taking today if the details aren't kept with them.

Photos in different places

Everyone has their own photos. Some are on phones, some on old computers, and plenty are buried in family WhatsApp chats.

Missing details

After a few years, it can be hard to remember when a photo was taken, who was there or what was happening that day.

The photos we take today

The pictures we take of our children now will be the old family photographs somebody looks through years from now.

Rooted Memories gives families somewhere to keep their photos with the names and stories that go with them.

How it works

A family tree with the photos attached.

Each person has their own gallery. Family members can add photographs, tag who's in them and look back through the years.

  1. Start with your family tree

    Add the people in your family. Each person gets their own place, with the photographs and memories connected to them.

  2. Tag people in each photo

    A photo of three people can appear in all three of their galleries. You only need to upload it once.

  3. Keep the details with each photo

    Add a caption so people know where a picture was taken, who was there or why you kept it.

  4. Add photos together

    Invite relatives to the same tree so everyone can add the photographs they've kept over the years.

  5. Look back through the years

    Photos are grouped by date, so you can scroll through someone's childhood, family holidays and everything in between.

How the free trial works

New Trees start with a 14-day free trial started through the App Store or Google Play. Your plan then begins automatically unless you cancel in your subscription settings before the trial ends.

If you decide not to subscribe, the Tree becomes read-only when the trial ends and is deleted 30 days later. You can subscribe at any point before then to keep it.

Photographs are stored at a size made for viewing in the app, so it is worth keeping important originals too. The full details are in the Terms of Service.

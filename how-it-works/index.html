How it works

How Rooted Memories works

Rooted Memories gives your family one private place to build a family tree, add photos and stories, and keep everything attached to the right people. The point is simple: instead of memories being scattered, they stay organised properly in one archive your family builds together.

What starts the process

You start by creating your tree. Each person has their own place, so the structure is there before the memories start piling up.

That matters because most families already have the photos. What they do not have is a clean way to keep them connected to the right people over time.

What makes it different

  • It is private and invite-only, not another public social platform.
  • Memories are organised by person, not buried in one endless feed.
  • Family members can build it together instead of one person trying to hold everything.
  • It is built to preserve family history, not just store files.

The process

1. Build your tree

Create your family tree and add the people who make up your family. Each person has their own place in the structure from the start.

2. Add photos and stories

Upload memories and attach them to the right people. That gives each photo context instead of leaving it buried in a camera roll or lost in a chat.

3. Invite family members

Family members can join privately and add their own side of the story. Over time, that builds a fuller and more accurate archive.

4. Keep everything organised

As more is added, the archive stays structured. Memories remain connected to the people they belong to, which makes them easier to look back through and pass down later.

Why this matters

Having access to thousands of old photos is not the same as having a clear family history. Without structure, most of it becomes noise.

Rooted Memories turns that into something usable, personal, and easy to return to.

What it becomes over time

At first, it is a better way to organise family photos and stories. Over time, it becomes something bigger: a private family archive built properly, with the people and memories kept together in one place.

What this leads to

The end result is not just a cleaner photo collection. It is a family archive that keeps growing instead of getting lost across devices, apps, and group chats.

That is the bigger picture behind Rooted Memories: helping families keep their story in one place, organised properly, so it can actually be looked back on in the future.

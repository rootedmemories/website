Getting started with

Using the app

You can build your tree
at your own pace.

  1. 01

    Create your family tree

    Start with yourself and a few relatives. You can add more people to your tree whenever you like.

  2. 02

    Add photographs and captions

    Upload photos and tag the people in them. Add a caption if there's a name, place or story you want to remember.

  3. 03

    Invite your family

    Invite relatives to join your private tree. They can add their own photos and help fill in details you might not know.

  4. 04

    Keep adding when you can

    You don't have to do everything at once. Add new photos as life happens, or work through older ones when you have time.

How it works

How Rooted Memories works

The process is simple. Create your tree, add photos and stories to the right people, and let your family build it over time.

The process

1. Create your tree

Add the people who make up your family. Each person has their own place from the start.

2. Add photos and stories

Upload memories and attach them to the people they belong to, so each one keeps its place and context.

3. Invite family members

Family can join privately and add their own memories, helping to build a fuller archive over time.

4. Keep building

As more is added, everything stays organised around your family, making it easier to return to later.

Why it stays clear

Memories are added to people, not dropped into one mixed gallery. That keeps the archive easier to follow as it grows.

Why family involvement matters

Different family members hold different parts of the story. Inviting them in helps keep more of it together in one place, and in safe hands.

What you end up with

A private family archive that grows gradually, stays organised, and remains easier to understand than photos spread across different apps, chats, and devices.

How it works

What Rooted Memories is, and why families use it.

Rooted Memories is a private family archive. It helps families organise photos and stories by person, keep memories in one place, and build something that can be looked back on for years to come.

What Rooted Memories does

Most families already have thousands of photos. The problem is not taking them. The problem is that they end up scattered across camera rolls, cloud drives, group chats, and old phones.

Rooted Memories gives your family one private place to keep those memories organised properly, attached to the people they belong to.

Instead of a messy album, you build a living family archive.

Why it is different

  • It is private and invite-only, not public social media.
  • Photos and stories are attached to real family members, not lost in one long feed.
  • Families can contribute together, instead of one person trying to hold everything.
  • It is designed for continuity, not just short-term storage.

How Rooted Memories works

1. Build your family tree

Create your tree and add the people who make up your family. Each person has their own place in the tree.

2. Add photos and stories

Upload memories and attach them to the right people, so each photo and story has context instead of being buried in a camera roll.

3. Invite family members

Family members can join privately and add their own memories too, helping build a fuller picture over time.

4. Keep everything organised

Instead of one chaotic gallery, memories stay connected to the people they belong to and the family they are part of.

Who it is for

Rooted Memories is for parents, grandparents, and families who do not want their stories to disappear into old devices and forgotten folders.

It is especially useful for families who already take lots of photos and want a better way to organise and preserve them.

What problem it solves

Having access to a relative’s old camera roll is not the same as having a clear story of their life.

Rooted Memories turns scattered photos into something structured, meaningful, and easy for family members to look back through.

What Rooted Memories is building towards

Rooted Memories starts by helping families organise today’s photos and stories properly. Over time, that becomes something much bigger: a family archive that can grow over years instead of being lost across devices and apps.

The goal is simple: help families keep the moments that matter, in one place, with the people they belong to.

Your family’s story deserves more than scattered photos.

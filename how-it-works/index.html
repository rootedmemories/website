How it works

How Rooted Memories works

Rooted Memories gives your family one private place to create a tree, add photos and stories, and keep everything connected. The process is simple: start with the people, add the memories that belong to them, and let your family build it together over time.

Where it starts

You begin by creating your family tree. Each person has their own place, which gives the archive structure before anything is added.

That structure matters because it gives memories somewhere clear to belong from the start.

What makes the process work

  • It is private and invite-only.
  • Memories are attached to people, not left in one mixed gallery.
  • Family members can contribute together over time.
  • The archive stays clearer as it grows.

The process

1. Create your tree

Add the people who make up your family. Each person becomes part of the structure from the start.

2. Add photos and stories

Upload memories and attach them to the people they belong to, so each one keeps its place and context.

3. Invite family members

Family can join privately and add their own memories, helping to build a fuller picture over time.

4. Keep everything together

As more is added, the archive remains organised around your family, making it easier to return to and easier to understand later.

Why this is easier to return to

When photos and stories are connected to the right people, the archive stays easier to follow. You are not relying on memory, file names, or old folders to work out what something is.

What it becomes over time

At first, it gives your family a clearer way to keep memories together. Over time, it becomes a private archive your family has built properly, with the people and stories kept in one place.

Why the process matters

Rooted Memories is not just about storing photos. It is about keeping them in a way that stays clear, personal, and worth returning to.

The process works because it is simple enough to keep using, and structured enough to matter later.

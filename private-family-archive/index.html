Private family archive

A private place for the memories you do not want to share with the world.

Family photographs are personal. Rooted Memories gives the people you choose somewhere private to keep photographs, captions and stories together, without turning family life into public content.

A private family archive is a shared collection that belongs to your family, where access is limited to the people personally invited to it.

01Private by design

Not every family memory needs an audience.

Social media was built for sharing outward. A private family archive is built for keeping things within the family.

In Rooted Memories, your family tree is invite-only. Instead of posting childhood photographs for followers or strangers to see, you can keep them among the relatives you have chosen to bring into the tree.

02Made for everyday families

Give the photographs sitting in your camera roll somewhere to belong.

Parents take thousands of photographs as their children grow. Grandparents have pictures from years before. Other relatives hold different pieces of the same family story.

Rooted Memories brings those memories together around the people in your family, so a child's gallery can grow from their earliest days and older generations can sit alongside them in the same family tree.

03More than storage

A photograph becomes more valuable when the story stays with it.

Camera rolls are excellent at storing images, but they are not a family record. Over time, names are forgotten, context disappears and important photographs become mixed among screenshots and everyday clutter.

By keeping photographs with the people they relate to, adding captions and letting relatives contribute, your collection becomes something your family can actually explore and understand.

Built to be handed down

Imagine giving your children the story of their life, not just a folder of photographs.

The first birthdays, ordinary Sundays, grandparents, holidays, school days and little moments in between can gradually form a gallery of a life.

As the family contributes, children can eventually look back through their own memories and explore the people and generations around them.

For families who want to go deeper, the same structure can continue into older photographs, stories and family history.

Private archive vs social media

Sharing with family should not have to mean publishing to everyone else.

Social networks are designed around feeds, followers, reactions and public sharing. Rooted Memories has a different purpose: keeping family memories together in a space centred on your family itself.

There is no need to build an audience. The value is in knowing the people who matter can contribute, revisit the memories and keep them alive together.

Frequently asked questions

Private family archives

What is a private family archive?

It is a collection of family photographs, memories and stories kept for a defined group of relatives rather than published publicly. Rooted Memories organises that collection around the people in your family tree.

How is Rooted Memories different from social media?

Social media is primarily designed to distribute content to an audience. Rooted Memories is designed to keep family memories within an invite-only family tree and preserve their connection to the people they belong to.

Is it only for genealogy or old family photographs?

No. You can start with the photographs you are taking today, build galleries for your children and invite grandparents and relatives to contribute. Families interested in deeper family history can extend the same collection into older generations over time.

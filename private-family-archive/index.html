Private family archive

A family space that actually feels like yours.

Rooted Memories gives your family one private place for the photos and stories you want to keep between you. Add the people you love, invite the relatives you trust, and build it together as life happens.

A private family archive is really quite simple. It is your family's own place to keep the good stuff.

01What it is

One place for the family, without making it public.

You create your family tree and invite the people you want inside it. From there, everyone can add photos, captions and memories to the people they belong to.

It can be as simple as you want. A few close relatives sharing the children growing up, or a much bigger family adding years of memories together.

02For right now

Your children are growing up while your camera roll keeps growing too.

You take the photos because they matter. But after a few years, the best ones are mixed in with screenshots, receipts, work photos and everything else life throws into your phone.

In Rooted Memories, your child's photos can slowly build into a gallery of their life. Not something you have to finish or organise in one go. Just somewhere worth putting the moments you want to keep.

03For the whole family

Grandparents probably have photos you don't.

So do brothers, sisters, aunts, uncles and everyone else who sees your family from a slightly different angle.

Instead of those photos staying on separate phones, the people you invite can add their own. One family, with everyone bringing something to it.

Different from social media

Share with your family, not with a feed.

Social media is built for posting. Rooted Memories is built for keeping.

There are no followers to build and no need to put your children's lives on a public profile just so family can see them.

Your tree is for the people you personally invite. They get to be part of it because you chose them to be.

What it can become

Start with today. The years do the rest.

01

A gallery of your children's lives

First days, birthdays, holidays, funny little phases and completely ordinary days. Added bit by bit, they become something incredible to look back through.

02

They can see what the family has been up to and add the photos and memories they have too.

03

04

Private by design

The people inside your tree are there because you invited them.

Rooted Memories is not a public family profile. Your family tree and the memories inside it are for the people who have been personally invited to join.

That gives families somewhere they can be themselves, share the photos they would rather keep close, and still enjoy them together.

A few quick answers

Private family archives

What is a private family archive?

A private place where a family can keep photos, captions and memories together. In Rooted Memories, those memories are organised around the people in your family tree.

Who can see my family tree?

The people you personally invite into it. Rooted Memories is designed as a private family space, not a public profile.

Is this just for people into family history?

No. You can start with your children, your parents and the photos already on your phone. If your family wants to go further back later, it can.

Start with the family you have around you now.

Add a few people. Save a few favourite photos. Invite the family. It can grow from there.

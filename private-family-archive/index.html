Private family archive

Your family’s private archive

Rooted Memories gives your family one private place to keep photos and stories together. Instead of being spread across phones, chats, albums, and old accounts, everything stays in one place and is easier for your family to return to over time.

Why keep it private

Family memories are personal. They should not live on public platforms or be shaped by feeds, algorithms, or attention.

A private family archive gives your family a quieter, more appropriate place to keep what matters.

What makes it private

  • Invite-only access, so only your family can see your tree.
  • No public profiles, no followers, and no social feed.
  • Your family’s photos and stories stay inside your family.
  • Built for long-term keeping, not short-term attention.

More than storage

Storage keeps files. A private family archive keeps them together in a place that feels clear, personal, and worth returning to.

Rooted Memories gives your family one place where photos and stories can stay together, instead of being split across different apps and devices.

Built for family use

Families can add to it together over time. Parents, grandparents, and relatives can all contribute in one place, without relying on different apps or separate albums.

That makes it easier to keep everything in one place, instead of piecing it together later.

Built to stay with your family

Phones get replaced, accounts are forgotten, and memories often end up scattered.

Rooted Memories is designed to give your family one place to keep everything together for the long term.

What it becomes over time

Over time, it becomes a clearer record of family life, built gradually and kept in one place.

Private family archive

Your family’s private archive

Most family memories are not lost overnight. They disappear slowly across phones, apps, and forgotten accounts. Rooted Memories gives your family one private place where photos and stories stay organised, secure, and attached to the people they belong to.

Why privacy matters

Social media is not built for family history. It is public, fast-moving, and designed to be forgotten.

Important memories end up buried, shared too widely, or lost when accounts change or get deleted.

What makes this private

  • Invite-only access. Only your family can see your tree.
  • No public profiles, no followers, no algorithm.
  • Your memories stay inside your family, not on display.
  • Built for long-term storage, not short-term attention.

More than storage

A cloud drive stores files. A private family archive keeps meaning.

Rooted Memories connects photos, names, and stories together so your family’s history stays structured and easy to understand — not just saved somewhere.

Built for families

Families can contribute together. Parents, grandparents, and relatives can all add memories in one place, instead of sending them across different apps.

Over time, that builds a clearer, fuller picture of your family.

Built to last

Phones get replaced. Accounts get lost. Apps come and go.

Rooted Memories is designed to keep everything in one place long-term, so your family’s memories are still there years from now.

What this becomes over time

At first, it is a simple way to keep your family’s photos safe and organised.

Over time, it becomes something much more valuable: a private archive of your family’s life, built gradually and kept in one place.
